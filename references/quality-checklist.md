# Quality Checklist

Do not export until every item passes.

## Production-mode gate

- **Visual exploration:** visual impact, hierarchy, and brand direction are checked. Generated wording is clearly identified as non-final visual reference.
- **Hybrid publication:** a copy manifest exists and every publication-critical text layer matches it verbatim; Chinese glyphs, punctuation, English terms, and digits are checked at 100% size. The generated visual is not unnecessarily simplified to make it typeset-like.
- **Fully generated:** the user has accepted text-risk, and the deliverable is labelled as a concept draft rather than verified external material.
- No publication-critical text is clipped, overprinted, distorted, or below the readable size for the target channel.

## Facts and brand

- Every statistic, employer, instructor, schedule, price, result, and credential has a user-provided source or is marked `需确认`.
- For hybrid publication, the logo is a supplied asset or is omitted. A fully generated concept must not be mistaken for an approved brand asset.
- Palette, wording, and prohibited elements follow the selected brand profile.
- Do not fabricate people, company marks, testimonials, achievements, or charts.

## Layout and export

- Main promise is clear at thumbnail size.
- Reading order, contrast, line height, margins, and CTA space are clear.
- High-density content follows a table or long-form grid and has not been compressed by shrinking the type.
- Export size matches the requested channel and the editable source remains available.
