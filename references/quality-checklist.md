# Quality Checklist

Do not export until every item passes.

## Production-mode gate

- **Visual exploration:** visual impact, hierarchy, and brand direction are checked. Generated wording is clearly identified as non-final visual reference.
- **Hybrid publication:** a final copy manifest exists and every publication-critical text layer matches it; Chinese glyphs, punctuation, English terms, and digits are checked at 100% size. The generated visual is not unnecessarily simplified to make it typeset-like.
- **Fully generated:** the user has accepted text-risk, and the deliverable is labelled as a concept draft rather than verified external material.
- No publication-critical text is clipped, overprinted, distorted, or below the readable size for the target channel.

## Facts and brand

- Every statistic, employer, instructor, schedule, price, result, and credential has a user-provided source or is marked `需确认`.
- Every visible string is traceable to source material, a supplied brand asset, or a necessary structural label derived directly from supplied content.
- Rewritten copy has an `original → optimized` record; material facts and professional meaning are preserved, and potentially substantive changes are marked `需确认`.
- Every visible string is necessary. No unapproved or duplicate eyebrow, decorative English label, pseudo-program name, tagline, badge, section code, corner annotation, summary, or similar filler copy has been added.
- CTA, logo, eyebrow, subtitle, and other optional material are omitted when absent from the source.
- Generated backgrounds and decorative graphics contain no readable pseudo-text or factual-looking marks.
- For hybrid publication, the logo is a supplied asset or is omitted. A fully generated concept must not be mistaken for an approved brand asset.
- Palette, wording, and prohibited elements follow the selected brand profile.
- Do not fabricate people, company marks, testimonials, achievements, or charts.

## Layout and export

- Final dimensions and aspect ratio match the selected channel and reading context.
- Height follows the content when the channel allows it; fixed-ratio output has been reflowed or split without arbitrary compression.
- Every readable text element is clear at 100% rendered size in the target viewing context, including captions, labels, metadata, notes, table text, and CTA text.
- Every rendered text boundary is fully contained by its assigned text block, card or section, and the final canvas.
- No glyph is clipped on the left, right, top, or bottom; no readable text is hidden by overflow, crop, mask, or ellipsis.
- No text overlaps another text layer, icon, CTA, divider, or decorative element.
- Every text layer retains visible inner spacing from its container edges; touching the edge fails QA.
- Variable text wraps correctly, variable-height containers expand, and cards in the same row accommodate the tallest content.
- Main promise is clear at thumbnail size.
- Reading order, contrast, line height, margins, and CTA space when supplied are clear.
- High-density content follows a table or long-form grid and uses channel-appropriate expansion or multiple images instead of unreadably small type.
- Export size matches the requested channel and the editable source remains available.
