# Design Recipes

## 清晰课程

Use for small classes, single courses, and simple curriculum introductions. Use a large title, one promise, compact summary strip, and 3–5 points. Avoid decorative panels that duplicate hierarchy.

## 行业势能

Use for industry topics, projects, and outward promotion. Pair an authoritative title with a restrained scene or data texture and a small number of verified proof points. Avoid generic neon, fake terminals, fabricated logos, and unsupported big numbers.

## 编辑高级

Use for mentoring, consulting, international programmes, and premium services. Use grids, whitespace, refined type, and selective warm accents. Avoid generic gold luxury effects, excessive outlines, and stacked cards.

## 高密度说明

Use for curriculum details, course materials, comparison tables, and service pathways. Use a report-like hierarchy, strong labels, strict columns, consistent dividers, and a long-form or carousel format. Avoid squeezing copy into one poster or creating every paragraph as a card.

## Five-dial Design Read

State the chosen value for each dial before production: communication goal, information density, visual tension, asset dependence, and brand fidelity. The recipe changes layout and visual language; the brand profile controls color, logo, and copy tone.
